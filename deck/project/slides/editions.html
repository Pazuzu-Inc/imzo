One protocol, two approvers: pick by risk and budget

Edition 1: in the demo

Imzo Approver app

  • Android app with no internet permission at all
  • Camera only; key in the Keystore
  • Installs in minutes on a spare phone

Edition 2: roadmap

Imzo Device

  • Pocket device: small screen and a camera
  • Secure element holds the key
  • No radios, nothing else runs on it

Same QR protocol, same backend. Policy can ask for the device above a threshold.

Say this: clients choose. The app is what you just saw, cheap and fast, with an offline guarantee we can check in the manifest. The device is the highest-assurance option for big payrolls and treasury; it is a design, not built yet. Both talk only through QR, so the backend does not care which signed. Be honest that a phone is still a general-purpose OS; that is exactly why the device exists.
