What Imzo does not claim

Not solved

  • An approver who is coached into approving a bad order. That needs the hold and cooling-off rules.
  • The app edition runs on a phone, not a dedicated device with a secure element. The Imzo Device edition closes that gap and is not built yet.
  • No independent security review yet.
  • Version 1 signs up to 50 items per order.

Real in the demo

Keystore signing, the strict parser, signature verification, the audit chain and the QR protocol.

Mocked in the demo

Core banking, the user directory and the payroll data. Device enrolment (open, no attestation) and the bank backend, which runs on the demo laptop.

4:15 to 4:30. Say the limit before a judge does. The device cannot stop an approver who believes a fraudster, which is why it pairs with the hold rules. Roadmap if asked: the Imzo Device edition (dedicated offline hardware with a secure element), or a SIM-based key as in Estonia’s Mobile-ID; key attestation at enrolment; file transfer for batches above 50 items.
