The hardest questions

QuestionAnswer
Isn’t this just a hardware token?The trusted display is an old idea. New here: a QR-only air gap, totals recomputed on the device, maker-checker and an audit chain, in this market.
Why not passkeys in our app?Passkeys prove who approves, not what. If the app is compromised, its screen is too. Imzo’s screen comes from the signed bytes.
What if the approver is tricked?Imzo does not solve that. It pairs with the hold and cooling-off rules in the Central Bank’s draft.
Why a phone, not a custom device?Speed and cost for a pilot. The protocol is device-agnostic; next steps are a dedicated device or a SIM-based key.
Can a rooted phone fake it?It is a risk. Key attestation at enrolment would let the server refuse unverified devices; it is not built yet.
