Order lifecycle

Draft

Pending approval

Partly approved

Approved

Executed

Cancelled

From Draft: the maker cancels.

Rejected

From either pending state: a signed rejection.

Expired

From either pending state: time runs out.

Executed, Rejected, Expired and Cancelled are final. One approval is enough to go straight from Pending to Approved. A failed verification does not change the state; it writes an audit event and counts toward the device lock.

Backup: the state machine from the plan document. The device has its own small cycle: pending confirm, active, locked, revoked.
